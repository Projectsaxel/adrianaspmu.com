What Is Lip Blush?

Lip Blush is a semi-permanent cosmetic tattoo procedure that enhances the natural color and shape of the lips. Using advanced permanent makeup techniques, pigment is carefully deposited into the lips to create a soft tint, improve symmetry, and add definition.

The goal is not to create a heavy lipstick appearance but rather to enhance your natural beauty with a subtle, youthful flush of color.

The Lip Blush Procedure

Every Lip Blush treatment is customized to complement your natural features.

The process typically includes:

  1. Consultation and color selection
  2. Lip assessment and design
  3. Application of topical numbing products
  4. Careful implantation of pigment
  5. Final review and aftercare instructions

Most appointments take approximately 2 to 3 hours.

Healing and Aftercare

Read the full aftercare and healing guide for the day-by-day timeline and the signs that need a doctor instead of the studio.

After the procedure, your lips will go through several healing stages.

You may experience:

  • Mild swelling immediately after treatment
  • Brighter color during the first few days
  • Light peeling as the lips heal
  • Gradual softening of the pigment

Final results become visible as the healing process completes over several weeks.

How Long Does Lip Blush Last?

Lip Blush results typically last between 2 and 3 years, depending on:

  • Skin type
  • Lifestyle habits
  • Sun exposure
  • Skincare products
  • Individual pigment retention

Touch-up appointments may be recommended to maintain optimal color and vibrancy.
