What Is Lip Blush?

Lip Blush is a semi-permanent cosmetic tattoo procedure that enhances the natural color and shape of the lips. Using advanced permanent makeup techniques, pigment is carefully deposited into the lips to create a soft tint, improve symmetry, and add definition.

The goal is not to create a heavy lipstick appearance but rather to enhance your natural beauty with a subtle, youthful flush of color.

Benefits of Lip Blush

Lip Blush offers numerous benefits for clients seeking naturally beautiful lips:

  • Enhanced lip color
  • Improved lip symmetry
  • Better lip definition
  • Youthful appearance
  • Reduced need for lipstick
  • Long-lasting results
  • Customized pigment selection
  • Natural-looking enhancement

Many clients choose Lip Blush to restore color that has faded over time or to create a more polished appearance without daily makeup.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Lip Blush?

Lip Blush suits most skin types, but not everyone. These are the conditions we check in the consultation, before any pigment is opened.

Lip Blush is for you if

  • Have pale or uneven lip color
  • Want better lip definition
  • Desire a naturally enhanced appearance
  • Wish to reduce daily lipstick use
  • Want to improve lip symmetry
  • Prefer long-lasting beauty solutions

Lip Blush is not for you if

  • You are pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, or currently using isotretinoin. This is refused at both studios, with no exception
  • You take blood thinners, have an autoimmune condition, or a history of keloid scarring. These need written clearance from your doctor first
  • Your natural lip tone is dark or uneven and needs correcting first. That is Dark Lip Neutralization
  • You have a history of cold sores and have not spoken to your doctor about antiviral medication before the appointment

The Lip Blush Procedure

Every Lip Blush treatment is customized to complement your natural features.

The process typically includes:

  1. Consultation and color selection
  2. Lip assessment and design
  3. Application of topical numbing products
  4. Careful implantation of pigment
  5. Final review and aftercare instructions

Most appointments take approximately 2 to 3 hours.

Lip Blush Healing Process

Healing is not a straight line: the colour looks darkest before it looks right. Knowing the order stops people from panicking in week one.

WhenWhat you see
Right afterMild swelling immediately after treatment
First few daysBrighter color during the first few days
During healingLight peeling as the lips heal
Following weeksGradual softening of the pigment
4 to 6 weeksFully healed. This is the result you judge

Aftercare decides how much pigment stays. The full routine is on the aftercare page.

How Long Does Lip Blush Last?

Lip Blush results typically last between 2 and 3 years, depending on:

  • Skin type
  • Lifestyle habits
  • Sun exposure
  • Skincare products
  • Individual pigment retention

Touch-up appointments may be recommended to maintain optimal color and vibrancy.
