How Do Lip Blush and Dark Lip Neutralization Compare?

Both lip treatments at Adriana’s cost $550, but they do different jobs: Lip Blush is additive, layering color onto an already-even lip tone, while Dark Lip Neutralization is corrective, rebalancing dark or uneven pigmentation before any color is added.

Lip Blush or Neutralization First: How Do You Decide?

The real decision comes down to your natural lip tone. An already even tone calls for Lip Blush alone; a dark, cool, blue-grey, or uneven tone calls for Dark Lip Neutralization first, with Lip Blush added afterward only if you still want more color once the tone is balanced.

Adriana Souza Santos trained in Brazil, where color-correction work on deeper and warmer skin tones is a routine part of permanent makeup training, not a specialty add-on. Booking Lip Blush directly over a dark or uneven tone tends to produce a muddier result than starting with Neutralization first.

How Does a Lip Appointment Work, and Who Needs to Talk to a Doctor First?

A lip appointment includes a consultation and shade selection, numbing, pigment placement, and a perfecting session 6 to 8 weeks later, included in your price. Anyone with a history of cold sores (HSV-1) should speak to a doctor before booking, not after, since needle trauma can trigger an outbreak even years later.

Dark Lip Neutralization often needs a second session to fully rebalance the tone, discussed at consultation once your natural pigmentation is assessed. Full pre-appointment guidance, including the cold sore precaution, is on the aftercare and contraindications guide.

Who Should Not Book Lip Permanent Makeup?

Anyone with a history of cold sores (HSV-1) should talk to a doctor before booking, since lip tattooing can trigger a reactivation. Pregnancy, breastfeeding, active isotretinoin use, and being under 18 rule out lip permanent makeup entirely; diabetes, autoimmune conditions, blood thinners, and an active skin condition near the lips need clearance, detailed on the aftercare guide.
