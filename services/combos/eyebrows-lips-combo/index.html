Who Is a Good Candidate for Brows + Lips Combo?

Brows + Lips Combo suits most skin types, but not everyone. These are the conditions we check in the consultation, before any pigment is opened.

Brows + Lips Combo is for you if

  • Want a complete facial refresh
  • Have sparse or uneven eyebrows
  • Want more lip color and definition
  • Desire a low-maintenance beauty routine
  • Prefer long-lasting cosmetic enhancements
  • Want to save time applying makeup daily

Brows + Lips Combo is not for you if

  • You are pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, or currently using isotretinoin. This is refused at both studios, with no exception
  • You take blood thinners, have an autoimmune condition, or a history of keloid scarring. These need written clearance from your doctor first
  • You would rather space the two procedures out, each can be booked on its own
  • You have a history of cold sores and have not spoken to your doctor about antiviral medication before the appointment

Brows + Lips Combo Healing Process

Healing is not a straight line: the colour looks darkest before it looks right. Knowing the order stops people from panicking in week one.

WhenWhat you see
Right afterMild redness
First few daysTemporary color intensity
During healingLight flaking during healing
Following weeksGradual softening of pigments
4 to 6 weeksFully healed. This is the result you judge
