Which Permanent Makeup Combo Packages Are Available?

Combo packages pair two permanent makeup treatments in one visit at a lower combined price than booking them separately. Adriana’s offers the Brows + Lips Combo at $850, performed in a single session at either the Wilmington, MA or Salem, NH studio.

Brows + Lips Combo

Brows + Lips Combo pairs a customized brow treatment with lip enhancement in one package for balanced, long-lasting results that typically last 1 to 3 years.

Which Brow Technique Should You Pair with the Combo?

The combo lets you choose any technique from the eyebrow menu, priced $550 to $650, paired with Lip Blush. The same deciding factor applies as booking brows alone: strokes mimic hair, shading mimics makeup, and your skin type points toward one or the other.

Oily skin blurs fine strokes and holds shading better, so oilier clients often pair Powder Brows with the combo; clients wanting an undone look often choose Nano Brows or Microblading. If your lip tone is dark, cool, or uneven, Dark Lip Neutralization is the right starting point, booked separately rather than as part of this combo.

How Does a Combo Appointment Work?

A combo appointment covers both areas in a single visit: consultation and design for the brows, shade selection for the lips, numbing, and the procedures themselves. One perfecting session, 6 to 8 weeks later, is included and covers both areas, healed together instead of two separate windows.

What Does the $850 Include?

The $850 price includes the brow treatment of your choice, Lip Blush, both consultations, numbing for both areas, and one combined perfecting session 6 to 8 weeks later. It excludes the Yearly Touch-Up, a separate paid visit about 12 months later from $300 per area, and it can be split with Cherry.

Who Should Not Book the Combo?

Anyone with a history of cold sores (HSV-1) should talk to a doctor before booking, since the lip portion carries a reactivation risk. Pregnancy, breastfeeding, active isotretinoin use, and being under 18 rule out both treatments; unsure clients are often better served booking brows and lips separately, detailed on the aftercare and contraindications guide.
