How Do I Choose the Right Permanent Makeup Service?

The decision usually comes down to three factors: what bothers you most in the mirror, your skin type, and your budget. Oily skin holds soft shading better than fine strokes; a tighter budget favors one single-area service over a combo; and a specific concern, like sparse brows or dark lips, points directly to one family.

Clients who fill in their brows daily usually start with eyebrows. Clients unhappy with lip color, not shape, choose lips, including Dark Lip Neutralization for uneven pigment, while daily eyeliner wearers choose eyeliner instead. A consultation confirms the fit before any pigment is placed.

What Does Every Permanent Makeup Price Include?

Every posted price includes the consultation, design and mapping, topical numbing, the procedure itself, and a perfecting session 6 to 8 weeks later at no extra cost. It excludes the Yearly Touch-Up, a separate paid visit about 12 months out that starts at $300 and is priced by how much pigment remains.

Who Should Not Book Permanent Makeup?

Pregnancy, breastfeeding, active isotretinoin use, and being under 18 rule out any permanent makeup procedure at either studio. Diabetes, autoimmune conditions, blood thinners, keloid scarring, and an active skin condition in the treatment area all require medical clearance before booking.

These restrictions apply the same way across every category, since all five use the same pigment-implantation process. The full contraindication list and healing timeline are covered in the aftercare and contraindications guide.
