What Is Bottom Eyeliner?

Bottom Eyeliner is a permanent makeup procedure that places pigment along the lower lash line to create subtle definition and enhance the natural beauty of the eyes.

The treatment is carefully customized to complement your eye shape, facial features, and desired aesthetic. Whether you prefer a delicate enhancement or a slightly more noticeable look, we tailor every treatment to achieve balanced and flattering results.

The goal is to create a naturally defined appearance that enhances your eyes without looking overly dramatic.

The Bottom Eyeliner Procedure

Each appointment begins with a consultation and design process.

The procedure typically includes:

  1. Consultation and eye assessment
  2. Lower lash line design planning
  3. Application of topical numbing products
  4. Precise pigment implantation
  5. Final review and aftercare instructions

Most appointments take approximately 2 hours.

Healing and Aftercare

After treatment, clients may experience:

  • Mild swelling
  • Slight redness
  • Temporary darkening of pigment
  • Gradual softening during healing

Healing is typically straightforward, and following aftercare instructions helps ensure optimal results.

How Long Does Bottom Eyeliner Last?

Bottom Eyeliner can last several years depending on:

  • Skin type
  • Lifestyle factors
  • Sun exposure
  • Skincare products
  • Individual pigment retention

Touch-up appointments may be recommended to maintain the desired appearance.
