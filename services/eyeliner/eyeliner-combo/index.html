What Is an Eyeliner Combo?

The Eyeliner Combo treatment combines Top Eyeliner and Bottom Eyeliner into a single procedure, creating complete eye definition and enhancing the natural shape of your eyes.

By adding pigment along both lash lines, the eyes appear brighter, more expressive, and more defined. The treatment can be customized to achieve a subtle enhancement or a more dramatic eyeliner appearance based on your personal preferences.

The result is a balanced, elegant look that remains beautiful throughout the day without the need for makeup touch-ups.

The Eyeliner Combo Procedure

Every appointment begins with a detailed consultation and design process.

The procedure typically includes:

  1. Eye assessment and consultation
  2. Eyeliner style planning
  3. Application of topical numbing products
  4. Pigment implantation along the upper lash line
  5. Pigment implantation along the lower lash line
  6. Final review and aftercare instructions

Most appointments take approximately 2 to 3 hours.

Healing and Aftercare

Following treatment, you may experience:

  • Mild swelling
  • Slight redness around the eyes
  • Temporary darkening of pigment
  • Gradual softening as healing progresses

Following aftercare instructions carefully helps ensure beautiful, long-lasting results.
