What Is Top Eyeliner?

Top Eyeliner is a permanent makeup procedure that places pigment along the upper lash line to create the appearance of fuller lashes and beautifully defined eyes.

The treatment can be customized to achieve various looks, from a subtle lash enhancement to a more noticeable eyeliner effect. During your consultation, we will discuss your goals and create a design that complements your eye shape and personal style.

The result is a polished appearance that remains beautiful throughout the day without smudging or fading.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Top Eyeliner?

Top Eyeliner suits most skin types, but not everyone. These are the conditions we check in the consultation, before any pigment is opened.

Top Eyeliner is for you if

  • Wear eyeliner regularly
  • Want more definition around the eyes
  • Have difficulty applying eyeliner
  • Lead an active lifestyle
  • Desire a low-maintenance beauty routine
  • Want long-lasting makeup results

Top Eyeliner is not for you if

  • You are pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, or currently using isotretinoin. This is refused at both studios, with no exception
  • You take blood thinners, have an autoimmune condition, or a history of keloid scarring. These need written clearance from your doctor first
  • You want a softer, diffused look rather than a defined line. That is Smokey Eyeliner
  • You want the lower lash line done as well. That is Eyeliner Combo

The Top Eyeliner Procedure

Every appointment begins with a personalized consultation and design process.

The procedure typically includes:

  1. Consultation and eye assessment
  2. Eyeliner design planning
  3. Application of topical numbing products
  4. Precise pigment implantation along the upper lash line
  5. Final review and aftercare instructions

Most appointments take approximately 2 to 3 hours.

Top Eyeliner Healing Process

Healing is not a straight line: the colour looks darkest before it looks right. Knowing the order stops people from panicking in week one.

WhenWhat you see
Right afterMild swelling
First few daysSlight redness around the eyes
During healingTemporary darkening of the pigment
Following weeksGradual softening as healing progresses
4 to 6 weeksFully healed. This is the result you judge

Aftercare decides how much pigment stays. The full routine is on the aftercare page.
