What Are Nano Brows?

Nano Brows is an advanced permanent makeup technique that uses a digital machine and an ultra-fine needle to create realistic hair-like strokes within the brows. Unlike traditional microblading, Nano Brows offers greater precision and is often gentler on the skin.

The result is beautifully shaped eyebrows that closely resemble natural brow hairs while providing long-lasting definition and fullness.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Nano Brows?

Nano Brows suits most skin types, but not everyone. These are the conditions we check in the consultation, before any pigment is opened.

Nano Brows is for you if

  • Your brows are sparse, thin, or were over-plucked in the past
  • You want individual hair strokes rather than a shaded, makeup-like finish
  • You have sensitive skin: the nano needle causes less trauma than a microblade
  • You want a defined shape you do not have to draw every morning
  • You can keep the area dry and product-free for the first 7 to 10 days

Nano Brows is not for you if

  • You are pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, or currently using isotretinoin. This is refused at both studios, with no exception
  • You take blood thinners, have an autoimmune condition, or a history of keloid scarring. These need written clearance from your doctor first
  • You have very oily skin: strokes blur faster, and Ombré Shading holds better
  • You want the fullest, most makeup-like brow. That is Nano Combo, not Nano Brows

How the Nano Brows Procedure Works

Every Nano Brows appointment begins with a personalized consultation. We carefully evaluate your facial structure and discuss your desired outcome before designing the ideal brow shape.

The procedure generally includes:

  1. Consultation and brow mapping
  2. Selection of pigment color
  3. Application of topical numbing cream
  4. Creation of realistic hair strokes using advanced nano technology
  5. Review and refinement of the final result

Most appointments take approximately 2 to 3 hours from start to finish.

Nano Brows Healing Process

Healing is not a straight line: the colour looks darkest before it looks right. Knowing the order stops people from panicking in week one.

How Long Do Nano Brows Last?

Nano Brows can last between 1 and 3 years depending on several factors, including:

  • Skin type
  • Lifestyle
  • Sun exposure
  • Skincare products
  • Individual pigment retention

Periodic touch-ups are recommended to maintain optimal color and definition. See our yearly touch-up options.
