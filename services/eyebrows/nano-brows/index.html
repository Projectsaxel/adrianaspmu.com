What Are Nano Brows?

Nano Brows is an advanced permanent makeup technique that uses a digital machine and an ultra-fine needle to create realistic hair-like strokes within the brows. Unlike traditional microblading, Nano Brows offers greater precision and is often gentler on the skin.

The result is beautifully shaped eyebrows that closely resemble natural brow hairs while providing long-lasting definition and fullness.

Benefits of Nano Brows

Nano Brows offers numerous advantages for clients looking to improve the appearance of their eyebrows:

  • Natural-looking hair strokes
  • Customized brow shape and design
  • Suitable for most skin types
  • Less trauma to the skin compared to traditional microblading
  • Long-lasting results
  • Smudge-proof and waterproof brows
  • Reduced daily makeup routine
  • Enhanced facial symmetry and definition

Who Is a Good Candidate for Nano Brows?

Nano Brows may be an excellent option if you:

  • Have sparse or thin eyebrows
  • Over-plucked your brows in the past
  • Want a more defined brow shape
  • Desire a natural appearance
  • Have sensitive skin
  • Lead an active lifestyle and want low-maintenance beauty solutions

During your consultation, we will assess your skin type, brow condition, and aesthetic goals to determine if Nano Brows is the right treatment for you.

How the Nano Brows Procedure Works

Every Nano Brows appointment begins with a personalized consultation. We carefully evaluate your facial structure and discuss your desired outcome before designing the ideal brow shape.

The procedure generally includes:

  1. Consultation and brow mapping
  2. Selection of pigment color
  3. Application of topical numbing cream
  4. Creation of realistic hair strokes using advanced nano technology
  5. Review and refinement of the final result

Most appointments take approximately 2 to 3 hours from start to finish.

Nano Brows Healing Process

Following your procedure, your brows will go through a natural healing process.

Clients can generally expect:

  • Slight redness immediately after treatment
  • Darker appearance during the first few days
  • Mild flaking during healing
  • Gradual softening of the color over several weeks

Full healing typically occurs within 4 to 8 weeks.

How Long Do Nano Brows Last?

Nano Brows can last between 1 and 3 years depending on several factors, including:

  • Skin type
  • Lifestyle
  • Sun exposure
  • Skincare products
  • Individual pigment retention
