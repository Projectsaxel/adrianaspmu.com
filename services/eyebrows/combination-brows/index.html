The Perfect Blend of Natural Hair Strokes and Soft Shading

If you love the natural appearance of Microblading but also want the added fullness of Powder Brows, Combination Brows may be the perfect solution. At Adriana's Permanent Makeup, we combine both techniques to create beautifully balanced eyebrows with realistic hair strokes and soft shading.

Since 2017, we have helped clients in Wilmington, MA and now Salem, NH achieve customized brows that enhance their natural beauty and boost their confidence.

What Are Combination Brows?

Combination Brows is a permanent makeup technique that combines hair-like strokes in the front and body of the brows with soft shading throughout the brow design. This approach creates a natural yet polished appearance that offers more depth and dimension than Microblading alone.

The result is fuller-looking eyebrows that maintain a realistic and elegant finish.

Benefits of Combination Brows

Combination Brows provides the best of both worlds by blending two popular brow techniques.

Benefits include:

  • Natural-looking hair strokes
  • Soft makeup-inspired shading
  • Fuller and more defined brows
  • Long-lasting results
  • Customized brow design
  • Enhanced symmetry
  • Suitable for many skin types
  • Reduced daily makeup application

This technique is especially popular among clients who want natural brows with a little extra definition.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Combination Brows?

Combination Brows suits most skin types, but not everyone. These are the conditions we check in the consultation, before any pigment is opened.

Combination Brows is for you if

  • Have sparse or uneven eyebrows
  • Want more fullness than Microblading alone can provide
  • Like a natural appearance with subtle makeup effects
  • Want long-lasting brow enhancement
  • Desire improved brow shape and symmetry
  • Prefer a customized and versatile brow solution

Combination Brows is not for you if

  • You are pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, or currently using isotretinoin. This is refused at both studios, with no exception
  • You take blood thinners, have an autoimmune condition, or a history of keloid scarring. These need written clearance from your doctor first
  • You want a single technique rather than two combined. See Nano Brows or Ombré Shading

The Combination Brows Procedure

Each treatment begins with a personalized consultation and detailed brow mapping process.

The procedure typically includes:

  1. Consultation and brow assessment
  2. Brow mapping and design
  3. Pigment color selection
  4. Application of numbing cream
  5. Creation of hair strokes
  6. Addition of soft shading
  7. Final review and adjustments

Most appointments require approximately 2 to 3 hours.

Combination Brows Healing Process

Healing is not a straight line: the colour looks darkest before it looks right. Knowing the order stops people from panicking in week one.

WhenWhat you see
Right afterMild redness immediately after treatment
First few daysDarker pigment during the first several days
During healingLight flaking or peeling
Following weeksGradual softening of the color and shading
4 to 6 weeksFully healed. This is the result you judge

Aftercare decides how much pigment stays. The full routine is on the aftercare page.

How Long Do Combination Brows Last?

Combination Brows generally last between 1 and 3 years depending on factors such as:

  • Skin type
  • Lifestyle
  • Sun exposure
  • Skincare routine
  • Pigment retention

Routine touch-ups help maintain the shape, color, and overall appearance of your brows.
