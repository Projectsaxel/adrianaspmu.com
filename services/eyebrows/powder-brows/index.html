What Are Powder Brows?

Powder Brows is an advanced permanent makeup technique that creates a soft, powdered effect throughout the eyebrows. Using a digital machine, pigment is gently implanted into the skin to achieve a beautifully shaded appearance similar to professionally applied brow makeup.

Unlike Microblading, which focuses on individual hair strokes, Powder Brows delivers a fuller, more defined look with soft gradients and smooth color transitions.

The Powder Brows Procedure

Each appointment begins with a detailed consultation and brow mapping process to create the ideal shape for your face.

The procedure typically includes:

  1. Consultation and design planning
  2. Brow mapping and shaping
  3. Pigment color selection
  4. Application of numbing cream
  5. Creation of the soft powder effect
  6. Final review and adjustments

Most Powder Brows appointments take approximately 2 to 3 hours.

Healing and Aftercare

Read the full aftercare and healing guide for the day-by-day timeline and the signs that need a doctor instead of the studio.

After your treatment, your brows will go through a normal healing process.

Clients may experience:

  • Mild redness immediately after treatment
  • Darker color during the first several days
  • Light flaking or peeling
  • Gradual softening of the pigment

Following all aftercare instructions helps ensure beautiful and long-lasting results.

How Long Do Powder Brows Last?

Powder Brows generally last between 1 and 3 years, depending on factors such as:

  • Skin type
  • Lifestyle habits
  • Sun exposure
  • Skincare routines
  • Pigment retention

Periodic touch-up appointments help maintain the desired color and definition.
