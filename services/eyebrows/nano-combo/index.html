What Are Nano Combo Brows?

Nano Combo Brows combine the precision of Nano Brows with the fullness of Powder Brows. Using a digital machine, ultra-fine hair-like strokes are created throughout the brow, while soft shading is added to provide depth, definition, and a polished finish.

This technique creates a realistic appearance while offering more density and structure than Nano Brows alone.

The result is beautifully balanced eyebrows that look natural from every angle.

The Nano Combo Brows Procedure

Every appointment begins with a personalized consultation and brow mapping session.

The procedure typically includes:

  1. Consultation and brow assessment
  2. Brow mapping and design
  3. Pigment color selection
  4. Application of topical numbing cream
  5. Creation of nano hair strokes
  6. Addition of soft powder shading
  7. Final review and customization

Most appointments take approximately 2 to 3 hours.

Healing and Aftercare

Read the full aftercare and healing guide for the day-by-day timeline and the signs that need a doctor instead of the studio.

As your brows heal, you may notice:

  • Mild redness immediately after treatment
  • A darker appearance during the first week
  • Light flaking as the skin heals
  • Gradual softening of the pigment

Following proper aftercare instructions is essential for achieving the best possible results.

How Long Do Nano Combo Brows Last?

Nano Combo Brows typically last between 1 and 3 years, depending on:

  • Skin type
  • Lifestyle
  • Sun exposure
  • Skincare products
  • Pigment retention

Periodic touch-up appointments help maintain optimal color and definition.
