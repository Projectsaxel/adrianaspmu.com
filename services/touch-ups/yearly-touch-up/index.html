Keep Your Permanent Makeup Looking Fresh and Beautiful

Permanent makeup is designed to provide long-lasting beauty, but like any cosmetic enhancement, it benefits from periodic maintenance. At Adriana's Permanent Makeup, our Yearly Touch-Up service helps maintain the color, definition, and overall appearance of your brows, lips, and eyeliner so you can continue enjoying beautiful results year after year.

For over 7 years, we have helped clients in Wilmington, MA and now Salem, NH preserve their permanent makeup investment with professional touch-up services.

What Is a Yearly Touch-Up?

A Yearly Touch-Up is a maintenance appointment performed approximately 12 months after your original permanent makeup procedure.

Over time, pigment naturally fades due to factors such as:

  • Sun exposure
  • Skin type
  • Skincare products
  • Lifestyle habits
  • Natural skin regeneration

A touch-up refreshes the pigment, restores definition, and helps maintain the beautiful results of your original treatment.

Why Is a Yearly Touch-Up Important?

Regular touch-ups help preserve the appearance of your permanent makeup and ensure it continues to look its best.

Benefits include:

  • Restored color vibrancy
  • Improved definition
  • Extended longevity of results
  • Correction of minor fading
  • Consistent appearance over time
  • Protection of your original investment

Many clients find that yearly maintenance helps them enjoy optimal results for years to come.

Who Is a Good Candidate for a Yearly Touch-Up?

A Yearly Touch-Up may be ideal if:

  • Your permanent makeup has noticeably faded
  • You want to refresh color and definition
  • It has been approximately 12 months since your last appointment
  • You want to maintain optimal results
  • You wish to make minor adjustments to your existing design

A consultation allows us to determine the best approach based on your current results and goals.

The Yearly Touch-Up Process

Every touch-up appointment begins with an assessment of your existing permanent makeup.

The process typically includes:

  1. Evaluation of pigment retention
  2. Discussion of desired improvements
  3. Color matching and customization
  4. Application of numbing products
  5. Pigment refresh procedure
  6. Aftercare instructions

Most appointments take less time than an initial procedure.

Healing and Aftercare

Healing after a touch-up is often quicker than the original treatment.

Clients may experience:

  • Mild redness
  • Temporary color intensity
  • Minor flaking
  • Gradual softening of pigment

Following aftercare recommendations helps ensure optimal healing and long-lasting results.
