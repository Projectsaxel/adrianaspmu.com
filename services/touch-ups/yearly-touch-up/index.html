Keep Your Permanent Makeup Looking Fresh and Beautiful

A Yearly Touch-Up refreshes faded pigment on brows, lips, or eyeliner, typically scheduled about 12 months after the original procedure, once color has faded enough to lose definition. Pricing starts at $300 and depends on how much pigment remains, assessed at the appointment.

What Is a Yearly Touch-Up?

A Yearly Touch-Up is a maintenance appointment performed approximately 12 months after your original permanent makeup procedure, once pigment has visibly faded. It is a separate, paid visit, not the perfecting session that is already included in your original price.

Over time, pigment naturally fades due to factors such as:

  • Sun exposure
  • Skin type
  • Skincare products
  • Lifestyle habits
  • Natural skin regeneration

A touch-up refreshes the pigment, restores definition, and helps maintain the beautiful results of your original treatment.

What Is the Difference Between the Perfecting Session and the Yearly Touch-Up?

The perfecting session is included in every original price and happens 6 to 8 weeks after your first appointment, correcting patchy or light spots once healing finishes. The Yearly Touch-Up is a separate, later, paid visit at about 12 months, refreshing pigment that has faded through normal wear rather than correcting early healing.

Clients often confuse the two since both involve revisiting existing work: the perfecting session corrects unpredictable healing, while the Yearly Touch-Up refreshes pigment that has simply faded with time.

A Yearly Touch-Up costs less than repeating the full original treatment, since the shape and color are already established.

Why Is a Yearly Touch-Up Important?

Regular touch-ups preserve color, definition, and shape before fading becomes noticeable, and skipping several years usually means more correction work at a higher price.

Benefits include:

  • Restored color vibrancy
  • Improved definition
  • Extended longevity of results
  • Correction of minor fading
  • Consistent appearance over time
  • Protection of your original investment

Many clients find that yearly maintenance helps them enjoy optimal results for years to come.

Who Is a Good Candidate for Yearly Touch-Up?

Yearly Touch-Up suits most skin types, but not everyone. These are the conditions we check in the consultation, before any pigment is opened.

Yearly Touch-Up is for you if

  • Your permanent makeup has noticeably faded
  • You want to refresh color and definition
  • It has been approximately 12 months since your last appointment
  • You wish to make minor adjustments to your existing design

Yearly Touch-Up is not for you if

  • You are pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, or currently using isotretinoin. This is refused at both studios, with no exception
  • You take blood thinners, have an autoimmune condition, or a history of keloid scarring. These need written clearance from your doctor first
  • Your original work was done elsewhere and has not been seen by the studio. That needs a consultation first

What Happens During a Yearly Touch-Up Appointment?

A Yearly Touch-Up begins with evaluating how much pigment remains, since that determines the final price starting at $300, followed by color matching, numbing, and the refresh itself.

The process typically includes:

  1. Evaluation of pigment retention
  2. Discussion of desired improvements
  3. Color matching and customization
  4. Application of numbing products
  5. Pigment refresh procedure
  6. Aftercare instructions

Most appointments take less time than an initial procedure.

Yearly Touch-Up Healing Process

Healing is not a straight line: the colour looks darkest before it looks right. Knowing the order stops people from panicking in week one.
