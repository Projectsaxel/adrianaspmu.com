No fixed, ongoing discount runs on this page. How Can Combo Packages Save Me Money?

Two combo packages cost less than booking the same services separately: the Eyeliner Combo at $500 versus Top and Bottom Eyeliner booked apart at $600, and the Brows + Lips Combo at $850 versus $1,100–$1,200 for the same two procedures booked one at a time. Combos also mean one healing period instead of two.

What's Already Included in Every Price?

A perfecting session is included in every original price, scheduled 6 to 8 weeks after the first appointment to correct patchy or light spots once healing finishes. It is not billed separately, and it is not the same appointment as the paid Yearly Touch-Up that follows about 12 months later.

A Yearly Touch-Up, starting at $300, also costs less than repeating the full original treatment, since the shape and color are already established and only refreshing is needed.

How Do Payment Plans Work?

Permanent makeup services can be split into payments through Cherry Technologies, Inc., issued by its own lending partners rather than by Adriana's Permanent Makeup. Cherry is a financial technology company, not a bank or a lender, and approval, terms, and eligibility are decided by Cherry, not guaranteed.

Training tuition at the Academy uses a separate in-house payment plan instead of Cherry; details are on the payment plans page.

Are There Seasonal Promotions?

Seasonal promotions are occasionally announced on the studio's Instagram, Facebook, or email list rather than promised on this page, since a promotion that has ended but stays live online is misleading to anyone who finds it later. Following those channels is the reliable way to hear about one when it runs.
