The Art of Effortless Beauty

What Is Permanent Makeup?

Permanent makeup, also called cosmetic tattooing or micropigmentation, places color pigment beneath the skin to enhance brows, lips, or eyeliner. Results last 1 to 3 years and save time on daily makeup.

PMU procedures use single-use sterile needles. Adriana holds Body Art Practitioner License 20261923 from the Wilmington Board of Health, where the studio holds Body Art Facility License 20261921, and in New Hampshire holds State Body Artist License 4283 (OPLC) and Town of Salem license BODA-10 under Salem Chapter 433.

Your Visit

How Does a Permanent Makeup Appointment Work?

A visit starts with a consultation and skin assessment, moves through design approval, numbing, and pigment placement, and finishes with a perfecting session 6 to 8 weeks later, included in your original price. Full healing takes about 4 weeks.

  1. Consultation and mapping, offered in English, Portuguese or Spanish
  2. Design approval before any pigment is placed
  3. Numbing, then pigment placement with single-use sterile needles
  4. Perfecting session 6 to 8 weeks later, included in the original price
  5. Yearly Touch-Up about 12 months out, booked separately from $300

Full healing timeline, aftercare instructions, and contraindications are covered in the aftercare guide, which every client reviews before booking.
