Permanent makeup is a cosmetic tattoo. Some conditions, medications and treatments make a procedure unsafe or make results unpredictable — including pregnancy and breastfeeding, certain skin conditions in the treatment area, some autoimmune conditions, blood thinners, recent isotretinoin use, and recent facial treatments such as chemical peels, laser or botulinum toxin.

You are responsible for completing our intake and consent forms accurately and completely, and for telling your artist about anything relevant before work begins. If we determine that a procedure is not appropriate for you, we may decline to perform it. You must be 18 or older to book a procedure.

Results, healing and touch-ups

Permanent makeup results vary from person to person. How pigment settles and how long it lasts depend on your skin type, your immune response, your aftercare, sun exposure and other factors that are outside our control. Photographs on this site show real results from real clients and are examples, not a promise of the same outcome for you.

Most techniques need a follow-up session to reach the finished result. Following the aftercare instructions your artist gives you is part of the result: work that is not cared for as instructed may heal unevenly, and correcting that may require an additional paid session.
