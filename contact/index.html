What Happens After I Send the Contact Form?

A message sent through this form is emailed directly to the studio, where Adriana Souza Santos or a member of her team reads it. A consultation, in person or by phone, is the usual next step before any procedure is booked or a deposit is taken. No specific reply time is promised here; call the studio directly for anything time-sensitive.

The consultation confirms technique, price, and timing against your features, since a message alone cannot map a brow or lip shape. Clients who already know what they want can book directly on Fresha instead of waiting for a reply.

What Should I Include in My Message?

A useful first message names the service you're considering, your preferred studio, and any past permanent makeup work on the same area. That detail lets the studio respond with an actually relevant answer instead of a generic one. Photos help but are optional, since the contact form below does not currently accept attachments.

  • The service you're interested in — brows, lips, eyeliner, or a combination
  • Your preferred studio, Wilmington, MA or Salem, NH
  • Whether you have existing permanent makeup on the same area
  • Any relevant health conditions, covered fully on the contraindications guide
  • Whether English or Portuguese is your preferred consultation language
