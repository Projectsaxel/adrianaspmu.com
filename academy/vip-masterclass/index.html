Why Is There No Fixed Price on This Page?

There is no fixed price because a single number would be wrong for almost everyone who reads it. A student who has never held a machine and a working artist fixing her lip blush healing need completely different classes, so the VIP Masterclass is quoted individually instead of listed at one rate.

Quoting one price for both students would mean overcharging one of them and shortchanging the other. So the VIP Masterclass is priced after a short conversation about four things:

  • Your current level, complete beginner, early career, or already taking clients.
  • What you want to learn, a full foundation, or one specific technique.
  • How many sessions that requires, which we tell you honestly rather than selling you a package.
  • Your schedule, since private training is scheduled around you rather than around a class calendar.

Who Is the VIP Masterclass For?

The VIP Masterclass fits three types of student: someone starting from zero who wants private attention instead of a classroom, an early-career artist whose healed results are still inconsistent, and a working artist who wants to add or refine one specific technique without repeating a full course.

Starting from zero

You have never done permanent makeup and want to learn privately rather than in a group, at your own pace, with the instructor’s full attention on your hands.

If you would rather learn alongside other students and want the accredited certificate, the 100 Hours Fundamental Class is usually the better fit, and the better value.

Early in your career

You are certified but your results are still inconsistent, or you are not confident enough to charge what the work is worth. Private sessions target the specific step where things break down.

Already working

You have clients and want to add or refine one technique, dark lip neutralization, nano strokes, ombré shading, without repeating a full foundation course you no longer need.

Does the VIP Masterclass Include a PMU License?

No. Like our other programs, the VIP Masterclass teaches technique and, for certified artists, refines it, but a certificate of completion is not the same thing as a license to practice. Licensing is set by the town or state where you intend to work, not by the academy.

Massachusetts has no single statewide body art license: M.G.L. Chapter 111, Section 31 gives each town’s Board of Health authority over its own rules, so a permit issued in one Massachusetts town does not transfer to another. New Hampshire licenses at the state level under RSA 314-A, and the Town of Salem, NH separately licenses under its own Chapter 433. Meeting the requirements where you plan to work is your responsibility, not ours.

How Do I Get a Quote?

Getting a quote takes one message or phone call describing your certification status, the technique or problem you want to work on, and your availability. We reply with an honest number of sessions and a price built for that specific request, not a generic package.

  • Whether you are certified, and where you trained if so
  • The technique or problem you want to work on
  • Roughly how many clients you take now, if any
  • Your availability, weekdays, weekends, or a specific window

If a group class turns out to be the better and cheaper option for what you need, we will tell you that.
