# Payment Plans for Permanent Makeup in Wilmington, MA & Salem, NH

You can split any permanent makeup service at Adriana's PMU into
 **4 interest-free payments** with Cherry, or spread it over up to
 24 months with interest. Applying takes about a minute and uses a soft credit check that does not affect your credit score.

Approval and rates subject to eligibility. Checking your options will not affect your credit score.

## How Cherry payment plans work at our studios

### 4 payments, no interest

Split your service into 4 equal payments, one every two weeks over 6 weeks, with
 **no interest**. Available for purchases from $35 to $3,000 — which covers
 every service on our menu, from $250 eyeliner to $850 combination work.

### Or spread it further

Prefer smaller payments? Monthly plans run up to **24 months**, with approvals
 of up to $30,000. Plans of 1 to 3 months are offered at 0% APR. Longer plans, 24 months
 included, are **not** interest-free: they carry an APR based on your credit
 profile, so you pay more in total.

### No hit to your credit score

Cherry runs a **soft credit check**, so seeing your options does not affect
 your score. You need to be 18 or older, live in the US, and have a mobile phone and a
 bank-issued debit or credit card.

## Estimate your payments

Enter the price of the service you have in mind and see what the payments look like before

 estimator do include interest, so check the total before you choose one.
