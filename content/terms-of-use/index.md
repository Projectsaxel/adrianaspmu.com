## Before You Book: Eligibility and Honesty

Permanent makeup is a cosmetic tattoo. Some conditions and medications make a procedure unsafe or make results unpredictable — including pregnancy and breastfeeding, certain skin conditions in the treatment area, some autoimmune conditions, blood thinners, recent isotretinoin use, and recent chemical peels, laser or botulinum toxin nearby.

Fill out our intake and consent forms **honestly and completely**, and tell your artist about anything relevant before work begins. We may decline to perform a procedure if we do not think it is appropriate for you. You must be **18 or older** to book.

## What Results You Can Expect

Results vary from person to person. How pigment settles and how long it lasts depends on your skin, your immune response, your aftercare, sun exposure, and other things outside our control. The photos on this site are real results from real clients, shown as **examples, not a promise** that you will get the same outcome.

Most techniques need a follow-up session to reach the finished result, and that session is included. Skipping the aftercare instructions your artist gives you can make healing uneven, and fixing that may take an extra paid session.
