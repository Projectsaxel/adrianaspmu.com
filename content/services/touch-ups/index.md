## When Does Permanent Makeup Need a Touch-Up?

Permanent makeup fades gradually and most clients schedule a touch-up about 12 months after their original session. A Yearly Touch-Up refreshes faded pigment on brows, lips or eyeliner starting at $300, and costs less than repeating the full original treatment.

The perfecting session and the Yearly Touch-Up are the two touch-up appointments at Adriana's, and clients confuse them constantly. The perfecting session is free and scheduled 6 to 8 weeks after your original procedure; the Yearly Touch-Up is a separate paid visit about 12 months out.

## How Do You Know When You Need a Yearly Touch-Up?

Most clients book a Yearly Touch-Up around the 12-month mark, once pigment has visibly softened, but the real signal is how much color remains rather than the calendar. Sun exposure, skincare products, skin type, and individual pigment retention all change how fast a given area fades.

Brows, lips, and eyeliner do not necessarily fade at the same rate, so one area needing a refresh doesn't mean the others do too. Waiting well past 12 months usually means more correction work, since more pigment has broken down; there's no fixed ceiling, but price rises with how much rebuilding is needed.

## How Does a Touch-Up Appointment Work?

A Yearly Touch-Up starts with your artist assessing how much pigment remains, since that sets the final price. Numbing and application follow the original process, lighter where color has held up well. The perfecting session, by contrast, is scheduled automatically 6 to 8 weeks after the original procedure, correcting rather than refreshing color.

## Who Should Not Book a Touch-Up, or Should Wait?

The same contraindications as any first-time procedure apply: pregnancy, breastfeeding, isotretinoin use, and being under 18 rule a touch-up out. An active skin condition on the area means postponing until it clears, detailed on the [aftercare guide](../../aftercare/).
