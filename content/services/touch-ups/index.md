## How Do You Know When You Need a Yearly Touch-Up?

Most clients book a Yearly Touch-Up around the 12-month mark, once pigment has visibly softened, but the real signal is how much color remains rather than the calendar. Sun exposure, skincare products, skin type, and individual pigment retention all change how fast a given area fades.

Brows, lips, and eyeliner do not necessarily fade at the same rate, so one area needing a refresh doesn't mean the others do too. Waiting well past 12 months usually means more correction work, since more pigment has broken down; there's no fixed ceiling, but price rises with how much rebuilding is needed.

The same contraindications as any first-time procedure apply: pregnancy, breastfeeding, isotretinoin use, and being under 18 rule a touch-up out. An active skin condition on the area means postponing until it clears, detailed on the [aftercare guide](../../aftercare/).
