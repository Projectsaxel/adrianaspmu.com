## What Is Microblading?

Microblading is a semi-permanent cosmetic procedure that uses a specialized handheld tool to create fine, hair-like strokes within the eyebrow area. These strokes mimic natural brow hairs, resulting in fuller, more defined eyebrows.

The technique is ideal for individuals who want to enhance sparse, uneven, or over-plucked brows while maintaining a natural appearance.
