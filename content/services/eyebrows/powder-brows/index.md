## What Are Powder Brows?

Powder Brows is an advanced permanent makeup technique that creates a soft, powdered effect throughout the eyebrows. Using a digital machine, pigment is gently implanted into the skin to achieve a beautifully shaded appearance similar to professionally applied brow makeup.

Unlike Microblading, which focuses on individual hair strokes, Powder Brows delivers a fuller, more defined look with soft gradients and smooth color transitions.
