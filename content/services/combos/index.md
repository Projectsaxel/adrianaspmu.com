## Which Brow Technique Should You Pair with the Combo?

The combo lets you choose any technique from the [eyebrow menu](../eyebrows/), priced $550 to $650, paired with Lip Blush. The same deciding factor applies as booking brows alone: strokes mimic hair, shading mimics makeup, and your skin type points toward one or the other.

Oily skin blurs fine strokes and holds shading better, so oilier clients often pair Powder Brows with the combo; clients wanting an undone look often choose Nano Brows or Microblading. If your lip tone is dark, cool, or uneven, [Dark Lip Neutralization](../lips/) is the right starting point, booked separately rather than as part of this combo.

## Who Should Not Book the Combo?

Anyone with a history of cold sores (HSV-1) should talk to a doctor before booking, since the lip portion carries a reactivation risk. Pregnancy, breastfeeding, active isotretinoin use, and being under 18 rule out both treatments; unsure clients are often better served booking brows and lips separately, detailed on the [aftercare and contraindications guide](../../aftercare/).
