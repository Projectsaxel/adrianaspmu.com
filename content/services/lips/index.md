## How Do Lip Blush and Dark Lip Neutralization Compare?

Both lip treatments at Adriana’s cost $550, but they do different jobs: Lip Blush is additive, layering color onto an already-even lip tone, while Dark Lip Neutralization is corrective, rebalancing dark or uneven pigmentation before any color is added.

| Treatment | Price | Job | Best for | Sessions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Lip Blush](lip-blush/) | $550 | Additive: enhances color, defines the border, corrects mild asymmetry | Lips whose natural tone is already even | Typically one, plus the included perfecting session |
| [Dark Lip Neutralization](dark-lip-neutralization/) | $550 | Corrective: neutralizes dark, cool, or uneven pigmentation with warm tones | Dark, cool, blue-grey, or uneven lip tone | Often more than one session |

## Lip Blush or Neutralization First: How Do You Decide?

The real decision comes down to your natural lip tone. An already even tone calls for Lip Blush alone; a dark, cool, blue-grey, or uneven tone calls for Dark Lip Neutralization first, with Lip Blush added afterward only if you still want more color once the tone is balanced.
