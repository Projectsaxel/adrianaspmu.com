## Lip Blush or Neutralization First: How Do You Decide?

The real decision comes down to your natural lip tone. An already even tone calls for Lip Blush alone; a dark, cool, blue-grey, or uneven tone calls for Dark Lip Neutralization first, with Lip Blush added afterward only if you still want more color once the tone is balanced.

## Who Should Not Book Lip Permanent Makeup?

Anyone with a history of cold sores (HSV-1) should talk to a doctor before booking, since lip tattooing can trigger a reactivation. Pregnancy, breastfeeding, active isotretinoin use, and being under 18 rule out lip permanent makeup entirely; diabetes, autoimmune conditions, blood thinners, and an active skin condition near the lips need clearance, detailed on the [aftercare guide](../../aftercare/).
