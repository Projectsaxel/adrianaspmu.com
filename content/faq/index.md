## Which Permanent Makeup Technique Is Right for Me?

Technique depends on skin type, how much definition you want, and how the area is used daily. Eyebrows split into strokes, shading, and combination methods; lips split into color work and neutralization. A consultation matches the technique to your skin and features.

### What's the Difference Between Strokes and Shading, and Which Suits My Skin?

Stroke techniques, like microblading and nano brows, draw hair-like lines and hold their shape best on normal to dry skin. Shading techniques, like powder brows, deposit soft, diffused pigment that holds up better on oily skin. Combination brows blend both. See the [eyebrow services page](../services/eyebrows/) for detail.

### What's the Difference Between Lip Blush and Dark Lip Neutralization?

Lip Blush adds a translucent wash of color and a defined lip line to lips without discoloration. Dark Lip Neutralization corrects a naturally darker lip tone first, before or alongside color. Both are $550 and include the perfecting session. See the [lips service page](../services/lips/) for detail.

## How Long Does Permanent Makeup Last, and What Does Healing Look Like?

Results generally last 1 to 3 years, depending on the technique, skin type and sun exposure; microblading sits at the short end, 12 to 24 months. Healing runs through several stages over about 6 to 8 weeks. Full day-by-day detail is in the [aftercare guide](../aftercare/).

### How Long Does Permanent Makeup Last?

Results generally last 1 to 3 years, depending on the technique, skin type and sun exposure; microblading sits at the short end, 12 to 24 months. Daily SPF slows fading, since color keeps breaking down under UV light. The yearly touch-up, from $300, restores the color when it's time.

### What Does Healing Look Like After a Procedure?

Healing runs through swelling and bold color in the first few days, flaking through about two weeks, and a hazy re-emergence of true color through week four. Full detail, including dry versus moist healing, is in the [aftercare guide](../aftercare/#healing-timeline).

### When Does the Color Settle Into Its Final Shade?

Color settles into its healed shade over roughly 6 to 8 weeks, when the perfecting session happens. Color often looks darker at first, then patchy, before the true tone re-emerges and the session adjusts anything uneven.

### Are the Needles Used Single-Use and Sterile?

Yes. Every procedure uses sterile, single-use needles and pigment cartridges, opened in front of the client and disposed of after one use. Reused equipment, not the pigment, is how infection spreads, which is why single-use needles are standard practice.
