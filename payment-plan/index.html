How Cherry payment plans work at our studios

4 payments, no interest

Split your service into 4 equal payments, one every two weeks over 6 weeks, with no interest. Available for purchases from $35 to $3,000 — which covers every service on our menu, from $250 eyeliner to $850 combination work.

Or spread it further

Prefer smaller payments? Monthly plans run up to 24 months, with approvals of up to $30,000. Plans of 1 to 3 months are offered at 0% APR. Longer plans, 24 months included, are not interest-free: they carry an APR based on your credit profile, so you pay more in total.

No hit to your credit score

Cherry runs a soft credit check, so seeing your options does not affect your score. You need to be 18 or older, live in the US, and have a mobile phone and a bank-issued debit or credit card.
